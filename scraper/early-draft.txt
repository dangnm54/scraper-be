Idea: Customer Support Automation for airbnb host

___Context:
- host usually have an excel file of FAQ and related answers
- host usually marketing their property on multiple channel: facebook, messenger, tiktok, airbnb
- they need to manually answer their potential clients


___Function & Tech:
- we have an webapp (runs on website) where host can
   + connect to those 4 channels
   + have a place to store all FAQ and answers

- the app will automatically answer clients using
   + AI to understand user's questions
   + map those questions with store FAQ
   + pick the suitable answers to answer the client
   + doing all of that while maintaining related tone of voice
   + signal to users (through phone / email / app) whenever the app don't know the answer to customers' questions

- users can configure
   + list of FAQ and answers
   + tone of voice of AI
   + information about their properties

- a level lower / easier to make can be
   + the app pre-type answer in the input box, users only needs to press sent
   + or the app pre-sent the answer to the user, user only needs to press sent

- the automation can ultilize the n8n open source to build it up
